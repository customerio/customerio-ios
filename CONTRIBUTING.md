# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in the Customer.io iOS SDK!

Code contributions are limited to the Customer.io mobile team, so we don't accept pull requests from outside the team. Your feedback still shapes the SDK:

- **Found a bug or have an idea?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/customerio/customerio-ios/issues/new/choose) and we're happy to look into it and make the change.
- **Need help with your account or integration?** Contact [Customer.io support](https://customer.io/contact).

Please follow our [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in issues and discussions.

Team members: see the [development instructions](docs/dev-notes/DEVELOPMENT.md) to set up your environment.
